Simulator: enter transactions, get a verdict and the working

Your transactions are added to a background-only synthetic week (the same generator the model was trained on), so rank normalisation and thresholds mean what they did in training. The model is a logistic regression trained on synthetic wash rings, hard negatives and decoys. It is a demonstration of the pipeline, not a real-world detector. After you press Score, click through the walkthrough to see your graph form, how each edge is scored, the cutoff, and which rings are found.
